KuloWargi

View on GitHub ↗

An offline-first mobile app for mapping and exploring your family tree across generations.

I built KuloWargi to make it easier to see how relatives connect as a family tree grows. The app pairs a canvas for exploring branches with a searchable directory. You can find a relative by name and jump straight to their place in the tree, or edit a member’s details and relationships in the app.

A custom layout engine arranges relatives by generation and keeps partners together. You can fine-tune the layout by moving cards yourself. The app stores the tree on your device and lets you export it as JSON for backup or transfer.

  • React Native
  • Expo
  • TypeScript
  • SVG
  • AsyncStorage

Features

Explore the tree

Pan and pinch to zoom across generations, drag cards into position, or search the directory and jump straight to a family member.

Automatic layout

A custom layout groups partners, orders relatives by generation, and routes parent–child lines around cards. You can adjust positions manually and snap cards to a grid.

Keep records on device

Family records and card positions are saved locally. JSON export and restore lets you back up the tree or move it to another device without a cloud account.

EYESEC Web Viewer Offline

View on GitHub ↗

A browser viewer for compatible EYESEC cameras on your local network.

The viewer runs on your computer and reads the camera’s RTSP feed over Ethernet or Wi-Fi. Once configured, it works during internet outages as long as the local network is available. Video travels directly from the camera to the viewer without a cloud relay.

OpenCV reads the camera feed, Flask serves the video to the browser, and FFmpeg converts audio for browser playback when requested.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • OpenCV
  • FFmpeg
  • RTSP

Features

Viewing controls

Zoom in for detail, pan around the feed, or switch to fullscreen. On compatible cameras, choose HIGH for detail or LOW for a lighter stream.

Audio and diagnostics

Adjust audio volume and boost quiet feeds. Monitor the delivered frame rate and TCP connection time to the camera.
