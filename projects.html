KuloWargi

An offline-first mobile app for mapping and exploring your family tree across generations.

  • React Native
  • Expo
  • TypeScript
  • SVG
  • AsyncStorage
View on GitHub ↗

Explore the tree

Pan and pinch to zoom across generations, drag cards into position, or search the directory and jump straight to a family member.

Automatic layout

A custom layout groups partners, orders relatives by generation, and routes parent–child lines around cards. You can adjust positions manually and snap cards to a grid.

Keep records on device

Family records and card positions are saved locally. JSON export and restore lets you back up the tree or move it to another device without a cloud account.
